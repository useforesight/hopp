Meeting mode, the faces beside the shared screen, and call windows that behave like windows.

## Installing

If you're on 1.1.0 or later, there's nothing to do. Hopp updates itself once you're not on a call and its window isn't in front.

If you're on stock Hopp, or the update doesn't arrive, paste this into Terminal. It quits Hopp, installs the new version and reopens it:

```
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/useforesight/hopp/main/install.sh | bash
```

You stay signed in. Coming from stock Hopp, macOS asks for Hopp's permissions again.

## What's new

- **Meeting mode.** Faces fill the camera window as wide tiles, not small squares. A button in its corner switches between a grid, with everyone the same size, and a speaker view, with whoever is talking big and everyone else in a row below.
- **The faces go beside the shared screen.** While someone shares, everyone's faces sit down the side of their screen, in the same window, and the separate faces window is put away until the share ends. Settings can put them along the bottom, in small bubbles over a corner, or back in their own window. A button in the shared window's header sends them out to their own window, or brings them back, and Hopp remembers.
- **Full screen and tiling.** The shared window's green button now takes it full screen, or tiles it beside another window. Their screen keeps its shape, with bars either side if it has to, and clicks still land where you point.
- **New pinned styles.** Pin the faces (⌘P) and they become a strip of round faces, a small box of everyone, or only whoever is talking. Drag a pinned window and it settles into the nearest corner, and opens there next time.
- **Call windows settings.** A new group in Settings chooses where the faces go while someone shares, how the faces window starts, what it becomes when pinned, whether it stays on top of other windows, and whether a new share opens where you are.
- **The sharer is marked.** In the camera window, whoever is sharing has a green ring and a "Sharing" badge. Click the badge to bring their screen to you. Your own tile says so too while you share.
- **The shared screen comes to you.** When someone starts sharing, their screen opens on the display and Space you're using, rather than wherever the window was last time. If you can already see it, it stays where you put it.
- **The faces stop covering their screen.** At full size, the camera window is now an ordinary window, so clicking the shared screen brings it in front. Pinned faces float over everything, as before.
- **The usual keys work in the call windows.** ⌘H hides them, ⌘M minimises, ⌘W puts one away and ⌘` swaps between the faces and the shared screen. The call carries on. While you're controlling someone's screen, these keys go to them, as before.
- **The call panel names them.** "Open shared window" in Hopp's menu-bar panel now reads "View Sam's screen", with the sharer's name.

## Good to know

- Only you need this release to see these. Nothing changes for the person sharing.
- No server change is needed.
- The Linux client gets these in a later release.
