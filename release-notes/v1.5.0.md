Celebrate in a call: confetti, a gong, fireworks and a soundboard, seen and heard by everyone.

## Installing

If you're on 1.1.0 or later, there's nothing to do. Hopp updates itself once you're not on a call and its window isn't in front.

If you're on stock Hopp, or the update doesn't arrive, paste this into Terminal. It quits Hopp, installs the new version and reopens it:

```
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/useforesight/hopp/main/install.sh | bash
```

You stay signed in. Coming from stock Hopp, macOS asks for Hopp's permissions again.

## What's new

**Reactions**

- A party popper in the call window's controls opens the reactions. Whatever you pick plays on everyone's screen, with its sound, and shows who sent it.
- **Emoji:** eight of them. Each flies big from your tile, then bursts into a shower of itself.
- **Effects:**
  - **Confetti.** Tap it again quickly for a bigger burst.
  - **Gong.** Hit it again for a bigger gong.
  - **Flip the table.**
  - **Slow clap.** As others join in, it builds to a standing ovation.
  - **Fireworks.** Hold to charge, up to ten rockets.
  - **Achievement unlocked.** For everyone or for one person, with your own title.
  - **Disco.** A few seconds of mirror ball, lights and a groove. Press it again to stop it early.
  - **Ship it.** A countdown and a rocket launch with your own words, such as a version number.
- **Soundboard:** an air horn, ba-dum-tss, a sad trombone, crickets, a drumroll, cha-ching, a "wooow" and applause, also on keys 1 to 8 while the reactions are open.
- **Sounds:** the switch at the top turns them off for you. You still see everything.
- The reactions close when you click away or press Escape. Effects play over everything without taking focus from what you're doing.

## Good to know

- Everyone needs this release to see reactions. Anyone on 1.4.0 or older sees nothing, and nothing breaks for them.
- Each person can send 8 reactions every 3 seconds, so nobody can bury the call in them.
- The Linux client has reactions too, once you rebuild it from `main`. Mac and Linux play each other's. On Windows they should work, but haven't been tried.
- No server change is needed.
