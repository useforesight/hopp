Go off the record by saying so, and see when the wake word was heard.

## Installing

If you're on 1.1.0 or later, there's nothing to do. Hopp updates itself once you're not on a call and its window isn't in front.

If you're on stock Hopp, or the update doesn't arrive, paste this into Terminal. It quits Hopp, installs the new version and reopens it:

```
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/useforesight/hopp/main/install.sh | bash
```

You stay signed in. Coming from stock Hopp, macOS asks for Hopp's permissions again.

## What's new

**Off the record**

- Say "tinfoil hat" or "off the record" in a call and every capture in the call pauses for two minutes. Nothing said in that time is written to a transcript, and no stills are kept.
- Everyone sees a pill under the call with the time left, `+2 min` and `Resume`. Either button works for the whole call, whoever presses it. Each `+2 min` adds two minutes, up to ten.
- Five beeps count down the last five seconds, then a longer tone marks the capture starting again.
- The transcript gets an "Off the record" and a "Back on the record" line, and nothing between them.

**Heard you**

- When your capture hears someone say "Jeepers", a light runs round their tile and a bubble shows their words so far, a second or two after they speak. Before, a slow answer looked the same as a word that was never heard.
- Both go a few seconds after they stop talking.

## Good to know

- Someone in the call has to be capturing for the phrase to be heard. It takes about two seconds.
- Everyone needs this release. Anyone on 1.3.2 or older keeps capturing through the pause, and sees no pill.
- On a Mac, the countdown beeps may stay silent until you've clicked in Hopp's main window once.
- No server change is needed.
