Captures, stamps and coding agents on the Mac.

## Installing

If you're on 1.1.0, there's nothing to do. Hopp updates itself once you're not on a call and its window isn't in front.

If you're on stock Hopp, or the update doesn't arrive, paste this into Terminal. It quits Hopp, installs the new version and reopens it:

```
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/useforesight/hopp/main/install.sh | bash
```

You stay signed in. Coming from stock Hopp, macOS asks for Hopp's permissions again.

## Read this first

**Capture is on by default, so every call is transcribed unless you turn it off in Settings > Capture.**

- Transcripts stay on your own computer.
- Your first call downloads a 731 MB speech model.
- People on 1.2.0 see that you're capturing. People on older versions see no sign, so tell them.

## What's new

**Captures**

- A transcript of each call, one track per person, with pictures of what was drawn and shown.
- The capture button in the call window starts and stops it. Right-click it to read, search or follow your calls live.

**Stamps**

- Hold down on a spot on a shared screen and say your note. Your words appear as you speak, and it's stamped once you let go and they're written down. This needs the call to be captured.
- If you're muted, holding unmutes you until you let go.
- Or click a spot, type your note and press Enter. Esc removes it.
- Right-click a stamp to remove it, or anywhere else to clear all of yours.

**Coding agents**

- `hopp mcp` gives an agent your captures, and it can follow a live call and wake when someone says "jeepers".
- `hopp capture search`, `hopp capture follow` and `hopp ask` do the same from a terminal or a script.
- Settings > Capture has the MCP setup to copy, and the command to put `hopp` on your `PATH`.
- Agents can read captures but can't start one.

**Screen sharing and calls**

- Drawings stay until you right-click or stop drawing. Turn off "Persist until right click" to have them fade.
- A sharing toolbar with Draw, Stamp, Revoke and Stop.
- Each person on a call gets their own colour, and cursors are sharper.
- Mic, camera and end-call controls stay usable when the camera window is very small.

## Good to know

- Windows doesn't have captures or the `hopp` command.
- Revoke applies to the whole share, not one person.
