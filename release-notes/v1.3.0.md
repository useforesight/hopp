Statuses, so fewer calls go unanswered, and quieter speaker indicators.

## Installing

If you're on 1.1.0 or 1.2.0, there's nothing to do. Hopp updates itself once you're not on a call and its window isn't in front.

If you're on stock Hopp, or the update doesn't arrive, paste this into Terminal. It quits Hopp, installs the new version and reopens it:

```
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/useforesight/hopp/main/install.sh | bash
```

You stay signed in. Coming from stock Hopp, macOS asks for Hopp's permissions again.

## Read this first

**Statuses only appear once your server has been updated.** Until then the app hides them and works as before. This includes self-hosted servers: update yours to this release's backend.

## What's new

**Available, Away and Do not disturb**

- Set your status from the account menu: click your picture at the bottom of the left-hand strip.
- Teammates see a dot and your status before they call.
- Away still rings. The call button says "Ring anyway".
- Do not disturb doesn't ring. The caller is told, and the call goes in your missed calls. The missed-call card waits until you turn Do not disturb off.
- Hopp sets you to Away when your screen locks or after 10 minutes without input, and back to Available when you return. It doesn't change a status you chose, and it doesn't apply during a call. Change the delay or turn it off in Settings > Status.

**Calls**

- Each person on a call now has a small, fixed slot beside their name: a dim mic, green bars while they speak, or a red mic when they're muted. It replaces the growing ring and green border.

## Good to know

- Windows sets you to Away after the idle time, but can't tell when the screen is locked.
- The Linux client doesn't show statuses yet, and still rings for someone on Do not disturb. Their app turns the call away, so they're told it was declined.
- People on older versions of Hopp don't see statuses, and are told a call to someone on Do not disturb was declined.
