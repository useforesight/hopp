Fixes for statuses that stayed wrong, people shown in calls they'd left, and transcription restarting itself.

## Installing

If you're on 1.1.0 or later, there's nothing to do. Hopp updates itself once you're not on a call and its window isn't in front.

If you're on stock Hopp, or the update doesn't arrive, paste this into Terminal. It quits Hopp, installs the new version and reopens it:

```
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/useforesight/hopp/main/install.sh | bash
```

You stay signed in. Coming from stock Hopp, macOS asks for Hopp's permissions again.

## What's fixed

**Statuses**

- Starting or joining a call sets an Away you chose back to Available. Before, someone who picked Away and forgot showed as Away for days, through every call they took. Choosing Away during a call still sticks, and Do not disturb is never changed.
- Someone on a call no longer shows an Away dot in the teammate list.

**Phantom calls**

- Someone left alone in a one-to-one call no longer shows as "In a call", and has no Join button. Joining only put you in the call with them, usually because their app had kept it open by mistake.
- Once your server runs this release's backend, it closes a one-to-one call after one person has been alone in it for 10 minutes. This catches calls left open by older versions of Hopp, which don't leave empty calls themselves. Rooms are never closed.

**Captures**

- The transcription helper no longer restarts itself every time it unloads after a quiet spell. It used to report "The transcription helper keeps stopping" and move from the GPU to the slower CPU.

## Good to know

- The server change only takes effect once the server is deployed from this release.
- The Linux client doesn't yet clear a chosen Away when a call starts.
