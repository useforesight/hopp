STATE 1 · SOMEONE IS SHARING

The shared screen, with the call around it

Option 3, revised: behind the sharp screen is the same screen, stretched to fill the window and blurred, like TV does with a portrait video, with a little of the desktop showing through. Everything you do is on the left: whose screen it is, how to view it, and your mic, camera, share and hang-up. The right side is only faces. The separate call window hides while this is open, so faces never appear twice.

STATE 2 · VIDEO ONLY, NOBODY SHARING

Just the call window, floating over your tiles

No screen means no viewer. The call is the small faces window: pinned to the corner on every workspace, sized by the number of people, never tiled and never stretched. It uses the same frosted, see-through style, so it matches the viewer.

  • When someone starts sharing, the viewer opens as a tile and this window tucks away; when the share ends, it comes back.
  • In the viewer's default Follow mode there are no bands, so this window stays visible there too.
  • Hover it for S / M / L and column / row / bubbles; drag it to any corner.

Synthetic people and screen. The see-through look needs Hyprland's decoration:blur; with it off, the desktop shows through unfrosted.
