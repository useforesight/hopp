OPTION 1

Faces and controls dock in the bands

The leftover space becomes the call window: who's sharing and the view controls on one side, faces and call buttons on the other. Nothing floats over the remote screen, and the separate call window isn't needed while you watch.

OPTION 2

Ambient: a blurred, dimmed copy of the screen

The bands are a blurred copy of the shared screen, partly transparent so your wallpaper (or whatever is behind the window) shows through, frosted by Hyprland's blur. The window feels part of the desktop rather than a box with bars. Controls stay in the small floating pill; faces stay in the call window.

OPTION 3

Both: ambient bands with frosted dock panels

The same see-through ambient bands, with the info and faces on frosted panels sitting in them. Everything is in one window, and it still blends into the desktop.

In the real client the window would be transparent (GPUI supports it) and Hyprland would do the frosting, but only with decoration:blur enabled — it's off on your machine today, which is what "Blur off" shows. The shared screen is the same synthetic 2560×1600 editor used in the screenshots. Resize the page or switch the tile shape: everything is laid out from the window's size, as the real viewer would be. If a band is too thin for a dock (under ~90 px), it falls back to the floating pill.
