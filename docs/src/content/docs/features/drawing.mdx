Drawing lets you draw on your teammates' screens in Hopp 🖍️

If you try the drawing feature, please share feedback with us, so we can improve it 🙏

## How to use drawing as a Controller

When you are in a call, you can click the drawing button in the control panel to start drawing.

By default, the drawing is temporary: it fades away 3 seconds after your last stroke. Everything you drew stays until then, so you can draw several strokes without the first ones disappearing. You can change this by clicking the settings button in the control panel, and then enable the "Persist until right click" option. You can also see this in the above video demo.

## How to use drawing as a Sharer

You can also draw on your own screen while sharing:

1. Click the drawing button in the call controls to enable drawing mode.
2. You can configure the drawing mode from the three dots menu:
   - **Temporary**: Drawings fade away 3 seconds after your last stroke (default).
   - **Persist until right click**: Drawings remain on screen until you right-click to clear them.
3. To exit drawing mode, press **Escape**.

## Drawing controls

These work for both Controllers and Sharers:

- **Click** to place a dot.
- **Hold Shift** while dragging to draw a straight line.
- **Right-click** to clear everything you've drawn, in either mode.
- **Cmd+Z** (Ctrl+Z on Windows and Linux) to take back your last stroke.
